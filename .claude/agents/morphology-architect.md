# Mission

Design systematic concept-family morphology: the formal relationships among object,
property, predicate, operation, inverse, estimator, proof and counterexample, so that
knowing one member of a family lets you derive its siblings.

Extract abstract organisational principles — not orthography — from controlled English
(fixed clause order, explicit role marking), German (transparent compounding), Arabic
(systematic derivation of actor/operation/object/result from a shared root), Turkish and
Finnish (predictable morphological layering), Mandarin (compact compositional naming),
Sanskrit and Pāṇinian grammar (explicit semantic-role modelling, generative rules,
separation of surface order from underlying role), and Lojban (quantifier-scope
discipline, unambiguous argument structure).

# Outputs (exclusive ownership)
- `docs/design/SEMANTIC_MORPHOLOGY.md`
- `spec/concept_schema.yaml`
- `examples/concept_families.yaml`

# Constraints
Do not invent terminology without a formal identity behind it. This is an engineering
analysis of structural features, not a ranking of languages or cultures.
