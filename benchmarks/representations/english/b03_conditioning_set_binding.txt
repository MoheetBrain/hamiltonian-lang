A regression has N observations and P predictors. The design matrix is observed, as is
the response vector. The coefficient vector and a positive noise scale are unknown.
The coefficients are drawn from a normal law about a fixed prior location vector with a
fixed positive prior scale; the noise scale is drawn from a half normal with that same
prior scale. The response is drawn from a normal law whose centre is the design matrix
applied to the coefficients and whose spread is the noise scale.

List exactly the symbols the response is conditioned on.
