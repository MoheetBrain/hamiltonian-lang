\begin{definition}[Location--scale family]
A location--scale family is determined by parameters
$\mu \in \mathbb{R}$ (location) and $\sigma \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ (scale),
together with the standardising map
\[
  z(x) := \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}.
\]
It satisfies the invariants $\sigma > 0$ and $z(\mu) = 0$.
For example, $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ is the member with $\mu = 0$, $\sigma = 1$.
\end{definition}
