We have a design matrix x with N rows and P columns of real numbers, and a
response vector y of N real numbers. The coefficient vector beta has P real
entries and the noise standard deviation sigma is strictly positive. Conditional
on x, beta and sigma, the response y is multivariate normal with mean vector
equal to the matrix product of x and beta, and with each component having
standard deviation sigma. Infer the posterior distribution of beta and sigma.
