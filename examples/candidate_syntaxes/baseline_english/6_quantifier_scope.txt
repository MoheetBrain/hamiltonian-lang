First statement: for every real number x there exists a real number y such that y
is greater than x. This says the reals are unbounded above, and it is true.

Second statement: there exists a real number y such that for every real number x,
y is greater than x. This says some real number is greater than every real
number, and it is false. Note that only the order of the two quantifiers differs
between the two statements.
