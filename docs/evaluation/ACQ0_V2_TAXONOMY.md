# ACQ0 v2 development taxonomy — FROZEN

Twelve feature cells, twelve development tasks, two sealed spares. Generated from
`evaluation/corpus_governance/taxonomy.py`; `tests/evaluation/test_taxonomy_coverage.py`
fails if the corpus stops satisfying it. Do not edit this file by hand.

**Frozen 2026-08-06.** No previous taxonomy was found; this replaces the missing one and
is the authority from here.

## Why a taxonomy

Twelve tasks is not a specification. Without one, a corpus drifts toward whatever is easy
to author, and a surface can win by being good at whatever happens to be over-represented.

| 7 | dimensional algebra | rational-exponent dimension vectors, and their normalisation | `v2_dimensional_algebra_002`, `v2_dimensional_algebra_004` |
| 8 | transformation side conditions | the condition a rewrite depends on, stated rather than assumed | `v2_transformation_side_condition_001` |
| 9 | objective-bearing proof task | a `prove` goal, declared by an objective of matching kind | `v2_quantifier_scope_003` |
| 10 | objective-bearing check or construction task | a `check` goal discharged by a `decide` objective (D-031) | `v2_dimensional_algebra_004` |

## Coverage rules

Checked over the twelve development tasks only — a spare is a replacement, not
coverage.

| rule | required | actual |
|---|---|---|
| objective-bearing tasks | ≥ 2 | **2** |
| state_in_prose=True tasks | ≥ 2 | **2** |
| CheckStmt tasks | ≥ 2 | **2** |

| probability-role tasks | ≥ 2 | **2** |
| quantifier/scope tasks | ≥ 2 | **2** |
| both `state_in_prose` values present | yes | **True** |

## Fragility

A cell with one carrier is fragile: reject that task in review and the feature leaves the
corpus silently.

- **Backed by a sealed spare:** cells 9 and 10 — `v2_implication_equivalence_spare_001`
  and `v2_dimensional_algebra_spare_001` respectively.
- **Single carrier, no spare — a stated limitation, not an oversight:** cell 4 (two-sided
  approximation boundary) and cell 8 (transformation side conditions). HML has no
  absolute-value form, so cell 4's shape is narrow; cell 8 needs a side condition that is
  both expressible and non-vacuous, and the dimensioned version does not compile
  (HML-D502). Adding a second carrier for either is the first thing to do if the corpus
  grows.

## Two tasks were replaced during review

Both replacements changed the mathematics, so both took fresh ids. The superseded pair is
preserved with reasons in `evaluation/results/acq0/superseded_tasks/SUPERSEDED.md`.

- `v2_quantifier_scope_002` -> **`v2_quantifier_scope_003`**. Its premise
  `exists b : Real . forall x : Real . x < b` is FALSE in the reals: instantiate the inner
  binder at `x := b` and it asserts `b < b`. An inconsistent premise entails everything —
  including the converse the task explicitly prohibited — and its goal was a theorem, so
  the assumption did no work either. The replacement quantifies over `Probability`, which
  is **bounded**, so premise and goal both do work.
- `v2_dimensional_algebra_003` -> **`v2_dimensional_algebra_004`**. It named a definition
  `kinetic_energy` while omitting the 1/2. `0.5 * mass * speed ^ 2` compiles AND passes
  the dimension check, so a model that knows physics would be scored FAIL on a hash
  difference — the exact v1 defect. The replacement is the second moment of a point mass,
  `m * r^2`, which has no coefficient, so identifier and body agree.

## What this taxonomy is not

It is not a claim of mathematical exhaustiveness, and twelve tasks cannot support a claim
about population reliability. It fixes what the developmental screen measures so that a
surface cannot win on an accident of corpus composition.
